## Decision register and source baseline

### Decisions

Choices marked **Decided** were taken in review on 15 October 2026; **Open** items still block an interoperable release.

D1 **Asset identifier and state shape.** Decided: each Original is a did:vh, `did:vh:<SCID>`, whose log is its asset log under the Originals did:vh extension [S19]; there is no separate DID log and no `did:cel`. Each entry’s state is the full asset state, and the DID document is derived, with a `#files` service built from the resource descriptors. The Original gains a did:webvh alias, its own small log bound in both directions, when published on the web, and a did:btco alias when anchored (section 3, P3.6–P3.8). No Originals were issued under earlier formats, so none are converted. Open: the asset state schema, the `originals` method values and the exact DID document derivation, exact-checkpoint resolution and deactivation history.

D2 **Author key binding and key policy.** The baseline is one author per Original, for its whole life, whose identity-bound key signs each author entry directly, with no dedicated per-Original keypair; editors added by owner decision (21 October 2026) sign editor entries signed by editors’ own keys. Pre-rotation is on the DID side, not the asset log. The author DID is the asset log’s controller [S25]: author entries are signed by any assertionMethod key of the author DID that is not an updateKey of its own log, and rotation happens only in the author DID (section 5, P5.3). Decided (owner decision Q6, 19 October 2026): there is no separate Originals declaration in the author DID; the author’s key SHOULD be used only for Originals entries, which verifiers cannot check (P5.11). Decided (owner decision, 21 October 2026): the author grants editors, by DID, permission to change the name, the metadata or named resources; editors sign their own entries without the author’s countersignature; only author entries change grants, aliases, lifecycle or parameters, anchor or deactivate; grants and revocations take effect from the next entry (P5.15–P5.18). This needs VH-Log to permit additional state-only signers, by extending PR 11 [S25] or through PR 6 [S28]. Open: key custody, and per-resource grants finer than a whole resource.

D3 **Historical identity and ordering.** Decided (owner decision Q14, 19 October 2026): asset logs do not use VH witnesses, hosted or not (P9.8, P9.9). Off Bitcoin, an Original has claimed ordering only (P7.6); Bitcoin orders everything from the anchoring entry on (P9.10, P9.13), and the first valid anchor wins (P9.16). Witnesses were dropped because, once activated, they could not be removed and every later entry needed their threshold, so losing a threshold would freeze every Original using them, and Originals witnesses would be stateful services; anchoring already orders entries on the anchoring sat. It does not establish uniqueness: a competing anchor on another sat can exist undiscovered, so uniqueness is reported as not established and ownership as provisional (P9.16). Author DIDs keep whatever their own method offers, including did:webvh witnesses. Also decided: author DIDs may use any method with verifiable version history that the profile lists, and the baseline lists did:vh, did:webvh and did:btco, whose versions are inscriptions ordered by chain order, and an inscribed entry may name only a did:btco version that precedes its inscription (P5.8, P5.13); an inscribed entry is checked against the author DID as it stood at its inscription time, so later rotation or author DID deactivation does not make an anchored Original non-current (P9.15). Superseded: witness selection, independence, the witness declaration and the 60-second witness freshness bound; the Originals declaration was later dropped too (D2). Decided (owner decision, 21 October 2026): a did:webvh signer that moves keeps its identity only through moves its own verified log records, never through `alsoKnownAs` or redirects (P5.14). Open: how identity versions are packaged, and the freshness policy verifiers apply to author DID state (P7.8).

D4 **Entry state and package.** Decided: resource digests are SHA-256 multihashes, the only algorithm in the baseline set (P4.2); canonicalization is whatever the pinned VH Log version uses, and proofs use `eddsa-jcs-2022` or `bip340-jcs-2025`, permitted under VH-Log’s proposed rule for additional cryptosuites [S21] (P5.12). Open: entry state fields, asset log file names, multibase encoding, envelope discriminator, completeness declaration and limits. Publish real test vectors only after those choices.

D5 **Operational edge cases.** Decided: an update that leaves a digest unchanged does not advance that resource’s version (P4.4); an explicit null removes metadata, and the name cannot be null (P4.6); only Bitcoin settles a fork: until an Original is anchored, divergent valid branches are an unresolved conflict and none is current, whatever the timestamps (owner decision Q5, 19 October 2026; P6.6, P9.16). Decided (owner decision, 21 October 2026): a shared host accepts one append at a time by comparing the submitted parent with its head atomically, refuses others with a stale-parent conflict, and never changes a signed submission; the refused change is kept and reconciled into a new signed candidate (P6.7, P6.8); off-chain forks stay unresolved, and only an author’s anchor settles one (P6.6). Open: atomic local commit details, and whether to allow finer versionTime precision [S22].

D6 **Transport profiles.** Decided for the Bitcoin representation (owner decision Q15, 20 October 2026; P9.5, P9.13, P9.17): inscriptions on the anchoring sat carry complete entries as JSON Lines, with no metadata and no compact delta yet; the anchoring inscription carries the whole log from genesis through the anchoring entry, and later inscriptions one entry each, so Bitcoin alone verifies the log (owner decision, 20 October 2026, replacing Q15’s anchoring-entry-only inscription; P9.17); resource bytes are not needed for the log to verify, and only did:vh or did:webvh signer DID histories still come from elsewhere (P9.18); resources are not inscribed; an inscription counts at one confirmation, every report states its depth, and a reorganization that removes an accepted inscription is reported; the Bitcoin `src` kind is the anchoring sat’s did:btco alias; did:btco author DIDs follow the did:btco method v0.2.0 [S26]. A verifier compares the sat number and network of a did:btco DID with where its index reports each inscription, and does not accept placement that two indexes it uses disagree on (P9.10). Provisional: the media type `application/vnd.originals.vh+jsonl`, the one-confirmation policy, the 350,000-byte inscription limit, and the did:btco network prefixes `sig:`, `test:` and `reg:`, which this profile defines as the SDK uses them. Upstream ask: the did:btco method defines no network prefixes. Open: hosted discovery and relocation, Boop adapter changes, and any compact encoding (P9.14).

D7 **Later capabilities.** Decided: authorship never transfers. An Original anchors once, terminally, to one sat, from the local or the web layer, by an update entry that adds the sat’s did:btco alias (P9.10), and after anchoring no entry can add or replace a did:webvh alias (owner decision on #41, 20 October 2026; P9.10). Layers move one way: local to web, and either to Bitcoin (P9.10); an anchored Original needs no web alias for discovery or verification, because its did:btco alias names a sat that carries its complete log (P9.17). The alias it had at anchoring, if any, may stay; the sat’s holder is the provisional owner (P9.16), and transferring an Original means moving the sat, with the evidence that stays off Bitcoin handed over separately (P9.18). On Bitcoin a change needs an authorized signature, by the author or a granted editor, and an inscription on the sat, so a sale freezes the Original unless a signer and the owner cooperate (P9.10–P9.13). The first valid anchor in chain order wins, and a verifier reports its uniqueness as not established and ownership as provisional (P9.16). Open: whether to add resource-set changes, encryption or recovery. The anchoring sat carries the complete log; resource bytes and did:vh or did:webvh signer DID histories are carried off Bitcoin, in evidence packages and on hosts (P9.17, P9.18). No Originals were issued under earlier formats, so no migration or legacy verification is needed.
